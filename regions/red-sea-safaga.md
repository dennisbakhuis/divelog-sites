Safaga is a phosphate port 60 km south of Hurghada, sheltered behind the long island of Safaga
(Gezirat Safaga). It has always been the quieter alternative: fewer boats, and a set of reefs that
sit further out in open water and carry more current.

**Tobia Arbaa** in Soma Bay is a cluster of five coral pillars rising off sand, two of them split
in two, which is why the same site is also sold as Seven Pillars. **Abu el Kefan** is the serious
one — a 300 m reef standing alone in deep water with walls that fall away hundreds of metres,
plateaus at each end, and enough current to make it a drift.
