The province of Liège was quarried for *petit granit* — the blue-grey Carboniferous limestone that
faces half the buildings in Wallonia — and when the working faces went below the water table the
pits filled. **La Gombe** at Esneux and the **Carrière de Lillé** at Sprimont are two of them, run
as dive centres with platforms, lines and sunken objects at known depths.

They are cold, they are deep for their size, and they are the reason a lot of Dutch and Belgian
divers get their deep and technical training done without leaving the country.
