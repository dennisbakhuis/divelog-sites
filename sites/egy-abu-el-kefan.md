Abu el Kefan is a narrow reef about 650 m long rising out of deep water south-east of Safaga, 75 to
100 minutes out by boat. Its sides are near-vertical and drop hundreds of metres. At each end the
reef throws out a **plateau at 10–30 m**, and those plateaus are the dive: soft coral, large
gorgonians and black coral, with the current running across them.

It is an exposed site and a serious one — there is nothing to hide behind, the current dictates
the plan, and the boat has to come and pick divers up.
