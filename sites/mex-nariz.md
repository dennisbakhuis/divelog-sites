Cenote Nariz, also called **Otoch Ha**, is reached from the 54 km mark on the Cancún–Tulum federal
road and then about 8 km of dirt track into the jungle. Several other cenotes share the same track.

It is part of the connected **Sac Actun** complex. Maximum depth is 12.8 m. A **T-junction at about
7.5 m** splits the dive: roughly 610 m of line on the main right-hand passage, and about 518 m on
the left running towards Cenote Aktun Hu. The cave has fresh water over a halocline.
