A *loswal* is an unloading quay. This one is at **Dreischor** on the **Grevelingenmeer** in
Zeeland, 1.6 km along the shore from the Dreischor Gemaal pumping station, and it is a standard
Zeeland entry: a hard edge, with deeper water close in. The water is salt. The site is also listed as
Dreischor Loswal.
