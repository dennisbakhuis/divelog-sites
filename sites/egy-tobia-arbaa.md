*Tobia Arbaa* means **four towers**, and the same reef in Soma Bay is also sold by dive boats as
*Seven Pillars* or *Tobia Soraya*. The structure itself is **five pillars** rising from a sandy
bottom at about **15 m**, two of which are split by clefts wide enough to swim through, which gives
seven when counted generously.

The site is fifteen minutes by boat from Safaga. It is shallow and sheltered, with glassfish and
lionfish in the clefts, and it is a reliable night dive.
