**DiveWorld** in Enschede is an indoor dive tower: **10 m** deep, with warm, clear fresh water. It
is built and decorated in the style of a **cenote**, with rock formations and a cavern zone, so it
can stand in for a cavern environment during training.

Being indoors and heated, it is diveable all year, and it is used for skills practice, equipment
trials and courses, especially in the winter months when the Dutch lakes are cold.
