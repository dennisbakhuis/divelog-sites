**Coral Garden** is entered through the same sheltered lagoon as the Canyon, twenty minutes north
of Dahab, about 150 m away. Instead of heading out to the fissure, the route swims south from the
lagoon.

Coral heads and sand at **5–14 m** give way to a steep drop-off. The garden itself is a field of
isolated bommies on white sand in good light — the easy, undemanding part of this stretch of coast,
with the drop-off below for those who want depth.
