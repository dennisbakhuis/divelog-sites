**Malek 2** is one of the speedboat sites of the Brayka Bay dive centre, about fifteen minutes south
of the bay along a straight stretch of fringing reef between Nemo City and Marsa Egla. It is a
**wall and drift dive**, 5–20 m: coral structures and a pinnacle garden near
the reef wall, a gradual slope to the drop-off, sometimes a light current, hawksbill turtles.

It is a reef site, not a wreck, and it is not Sheikh Malek, a different bay north of the airport.
