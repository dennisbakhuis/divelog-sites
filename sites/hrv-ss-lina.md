## The ship

An iron steamer built at **Newcastle upon Tyne in 1879**, around 70 m long, 1 049 gross tons. She
passed into Italian ownership in 1901 and spent the next decade as a Mediterranean tramp.

## The sinking

On the night of **14 January 1914** she was running timber from Rijeka to Italy when thick fog took
her off course and she struck the rocky coast of **Cres**, near **Cape Pečanj**. She sank quickly.
It was a peacetime accident: the Adriatic's war was still seven months away.

## The dive

She stands **upright and close inshore**, bow at about **28 m** and stern down the slope at **53 m**
— so the depth you dive her at is a choice. The lower hull is holed where the reef went through her,
and you can still see remains of the **timber cargo** in the holds. The decaying cabins are
swimmable. She is regularly called the most photogenic wreck in Croatia.

**Position corrected September 2026.** This site was previously pinned to **Krk**, 23 km east. The *Lina* is on
