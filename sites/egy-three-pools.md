Named for exactly what it is: the reef flat here has **three natural pools** worn into it at a
depth of three to four metres, connected by shallow channels. Divers walk in, cross the pools, and
go out over the reef edge to the wall beyond.

The pools make the site suitable for a wide range of users — snorkellers and students stay in
them, divers pass through on the way to the drop-off, and at the end of a dive they serve as a
**five-metre safety stop** with a sand floor and marine life to look at.
