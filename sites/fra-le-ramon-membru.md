## The ship

A steam cargo ship, 80 m long and 11 m in the beam, built in **1873** by Gilbert and Cooper. She
worked under four names: **Elguezabal**, then **Ballesteros II**, then **Derwent**, and finally
**Ramon Membru**.

## The sinking

In early **June 1921**, just outside Cavalaire harbour, an explosion aboard sank her. All
**thirty-seven** of her crew were picked up by the steamer *Cabo Vilano*.

## The dive

She lies on her starboard side in sand and mud at **12–22 m**, a tangle of plating rather than a
recognisable ship, but a well-populated one, with morays, groupers and lobsters in the wreckage.
Visibility here is usually average at best.
