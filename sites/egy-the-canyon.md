Twenty minutes north of Dahab, entered through a sheltered lagoon in the reef flat. The route
crosses a shallow coral garden, reaches the drop-off, and finds a crack in the seabed that has been
roofed over by growing coral until it became a tunnel.

The usual entry — *the fish bowl* — is a gap in that roof at about **20 m**. It drops into a
chamber, and from there the canyon runs down and out towards the sea, narrowing, with the floor at
about 30 m at the far end. It is dark inside, shafts of light come down through gaps in the roof,
and there is a permanent pocket of exhaled air trapped against the ceiling.

Most divers do the canyon first and spend the rest of the dive in the coral garden on the way
back, which is the sensible profile. **Coral Garden** lies to the south of the same entry.
