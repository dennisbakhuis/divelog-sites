**Gubal Seghir** is the smaller of the two Gubal islands in the Strait of Gubal. Its north-eastern
corner is **Bluff Point**, where the current accelerates round the wall, and the slope below it
carries the *Ulysses* and the Barge — both covered in `egy-the-barge-ulysses.md`.

This is probably the same place as `egy-gobal-island` (Bluff Point, Gubal Seghir) under a second
name. The two entries should be read as one island group.
