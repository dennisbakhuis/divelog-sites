**Poseidon Garden** is the southern end of the **Shaab el Erg** horseshoe reef in the Strait of
Gubal, about 1.7 km from the reef's main marker. It is a shallow coral garden on sand and an
undemanding dive — the kind of place a day boat out of Hurghada takes people who have just finished
their course.

The site corresponds to Poseidon Reef in OpenStreetMap, node/271188407. It lies on the Hurghada side
of the Red Sea, not at Dahab on the other side of Sinai.
