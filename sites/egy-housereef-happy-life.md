The **Happy Life Village** sits on the coast about 5 km south of Mashraba, and its beach is the
entry for Golden Blocks. Like every hotel on that coast it has a piece of reef in front of it: the
entry goes straight out from the shore, over the reef flat, and the wall is right there.

That easy access makes it the typical house reef — used for check dives, shakedowns after a gear
change and an easy last dive before flying, with the wall below for deeper dives.
