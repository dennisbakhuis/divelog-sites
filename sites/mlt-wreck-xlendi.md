## The scuttling

The **MV Xlendi** was an 80 m roll-on roll-off ferry, sunk deliberately on **12 November 1999** off
**Xatt l-Aħmar** on the south coast of Gozo as one of the island's artificial reefs.

It did not go to plan. She **capsized while she was going down** and landed **upside down**. The
entire structure above the car deck — bridge, accommodation, funnel — was crushed under the weight
of the hull. What is left is a flat steel ceiling above the seabed, with the wreck's insides
inaccessible underneath it.

## The dive

She lies at **42 m** on the shore side and **44 m** on the seaward side — deep, dark, and past the
recreational limit, so a dive on her involves decompression. There is not a great deal to see by wreck
standards.

The *Karwela* and the *Cominoland* lie nearby and both sit upright at 40 m. The *Hephaestus* was
scuttled here in 2022.
