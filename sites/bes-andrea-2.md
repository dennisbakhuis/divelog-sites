**Andrea 1** and **Andrea 2** are adjacent marked sites on the road north out of Kralendijk, on the
sheltered leeward coast: a shallow terrace, a drop-off at about 10 m, and a slope of hard coral and
sponges running down past 30 m.

It is a typical Bonaire shore dive: divers park by the road, walk in from the shore, and the reef
starts immediately.
