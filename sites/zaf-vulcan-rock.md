**Vulcan Rock** is a granite pinnacle off **Duiker Island** at the mouth of Hout Bay, rising from
about 34 m to within a few metres of the surface. Because the top is shallow it grows a full canopy
of ***Ecklonia maxima*** — sea bamboo — and the dive is largely spent moving through a kelp forest
with the rock walls dropping away underneath it.

The water is Benguela-cold, low teens, and rich with it: the rock is covered in soft corals,
anemones and nudibranchs, with red roman and hottentot in the kelp.

Duiker Island itself holds a large colony of **Cape fur seals**, and the seals come out to the
pinnacle.
