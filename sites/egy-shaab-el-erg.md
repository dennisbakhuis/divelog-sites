Shaab el Erg is a big horseshoe-shaped reef, opening to the south, about 30 km north of
Hurghada. The shape is the point: the inside of the horseshoe is a sheltered sandy lagoon with
coral pinnacles standing in it, and it works whatever the wind is doing outside.

It has a more or less **resident pod of bottlenose dolphins** that use the lagoon, and a great deal
of the traffic here is people hoping to see them. Outside the horseshoe the reef drops away
properly and there are hard-coral ergs along the eastern arm.

The southern end of the reef is **Poseidon Garden**, described separately.
