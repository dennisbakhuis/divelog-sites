A long submerged granite ridge running south from **Similan Island 3**, in the southern part of the
archipelago, with rock outcrops breaking the surface along it that look like fins — which is where
the name comes from, not from sharks.

It is unusually big for a Similan site, long enough to be dived twice in different halves, with two
distinct sides: a canyon and swim-throughs on one, a sloping reef on the other.
