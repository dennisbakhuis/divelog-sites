The other outlet of the **Système de l'Ouysse** below Rocamadour, 1.2 km west of Cabouy. Where
Cabouy is a pool feeding a horizontal passage, Saint-Sauveur is a *gouffre* — a shaft — and the
dive is a descent.

A **temporary ban** currently applies to diving beyond the vasque.
