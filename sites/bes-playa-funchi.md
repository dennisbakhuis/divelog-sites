Playa Funchi is inside **Washington Slagbaai National Park**, the terrestrial park occupying the
whole north-western end of Bonaire. Getting there means the park entrance, the one-way dirt loop,
and an hour of driving, so the site sees few divers.

The bay is shallow and calm with a reef on each headland, and it is known above water as much as
below: large **green iguanas** come down onto the sand and the rocks and are entirely used to
people.

Diving at several Washington Slagbaai sites has since been restricted because of **stony coral
tissue loss disease**, and gear sanitising is now required.
