A *gemaal* is a pumping station, and this one discharges into the **Grevelingenmeer** at Dreischor
on Schouwen-Duiveland. It is one of the most-used dive sites in Zeeland: parking on the dyke, a
concrete slope into the water, and a bottom that shelves gently out to about 12 m.

Because the Grevelingen has had **no tide since 1971**, there is nothing to time and nothing to
fight. The trade-off is oxygen: the deep water goes anoxic in late summer and the life there dies
back each year.

What is here is the Dutch estuary set — sea bass, gobies, cuttlefish in season, lobsters in the
rocks, and enormous numbers of shore crabs.
