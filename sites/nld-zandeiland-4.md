The **Vinkeveense Plassen** were dug for peat from the Golden Age onwards — turf for Amsterdam,
leaving shallow water divided by *legakkers*, the long drying banks. Then in the 1960s and 70s they
were dug again, for sand: the **A2 motorway** and the **Amsterdam-Zuidoost** development both came
out of this lake, and took it down to **50 m** in places. The spoil was heaped into a line of
islands, the **Zandeilanden**.

**Zandeiland 4** is the one with a road to it. Every other island needs a boat; this one can be
reached by car.

The site has platforms, lines, sunken objects and a wall dropping off the island edge into the deep
water.
