A **slate mine** at Bestwig in the Sauerland, worked out and flooded, and the biggest diveable mine
in Germany.

It is not a cave. The passages are galleries: rectangular in section, cut by people, with the
extraction faces and the tool marks still on them, and pillars of rock left standing to hold the
roof up. There is no karst here and so no cave system.

Diving is on a **fixed guide line** through the workings, in cold, still, extremely clear fresh
water.
