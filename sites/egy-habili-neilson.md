Properly **Sha'ab el Nabaa**, known to divers as Nelson Reef and sometimes written Neilson. It is
a *habili*: the Arabic term for a reef whose crown stays **below** the surface — here just 2–3 m
down — which makes it invisible from a boat and historically dangerous.

The reef runs east–west, with a plateau about 50 m long and a drop-off to roughly 25 m. The hard
and soft coral cover has earned it the nickname **Little Elphinstone**, after the much bigger and
more famous reef further north. It is normally dived as an unhurried drift along the plateau edge.
