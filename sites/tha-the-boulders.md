**Boulder City** sits south of Similan Island 3, in the southern group. It is what the name says: a
scatter of very large granite blocks standing on a sand bottom, running from about 15 m down past
35 m, with soft coral in the gaps between them and current running through.

Being at the southern, exposed end of the archipelago it gets less traffic than the central islands
and more big fish passing.
