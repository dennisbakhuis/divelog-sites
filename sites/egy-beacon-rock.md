Sha'ab Mahmud is a large reef system enclosing a lagoon, west of Ras Muhammad at the mouth of the
Gulf of Suez. **Beacon Rock** is the coral outcrop at its southern end, and the name is literal:
there is a metal navigation beacon on it.

The beacon is there because of the *Dunraven*, which hit this reef in 1876 and rolled over into
30 m of water just off the wall. The wreck lies about **580 m** off the reef, so the reef and the
wreck can be dived on one tank. The wall itself has overhangs and a drop into the blue, and the
wreck is a short swim along it.
