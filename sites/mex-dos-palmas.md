Dos Palmas is one of the entrances to the **Dos Ojos** section. **Steve Gerrard** first laid line
here in 1991, running upstream towards Dos Ojos and downstream towards Nicte Ha.

For twenty-seven years Dos Ojos was a system of its own. In **January 2018** Robbie Schmittner made
the connection to **Sistema Sac Actun**, and with it Sac Actun became the longest underwater cave
system on Earth.
