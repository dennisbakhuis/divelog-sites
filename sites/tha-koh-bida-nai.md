**Koh Bida Nai** — *inner Bida* — is the smaller and closer of the pair, a few hundred metres from
Bida Nok. Same limestone, same wall structure, but with a sheltered bay on its eastern side, a
small **cavern** at about 8 m, and a ridge running out south covered in soft coral.
