**Bommenede** is a *werkhaven* — a construction harbour built to service the Delta Works and left
behind when they were finished — on the south shore of the **Grevelingen** at Zonnemaire. There are
east and west basins.

Shallow, sheltered, and full of the things that colonise concrete.
