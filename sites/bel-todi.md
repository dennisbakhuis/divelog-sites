**TODI** at Beringen in Belgian Limburg is an indoor dive pool: clear, warm, controlled, with
platforms and objects at set depths, built into the site of the **Beringen colliery** — one of the
Kempen coal mines that closed in the 1980s and which is now a UNESCO-listed industrial heritage
site.

Like DiveWorld in Enschede, it serves as a place to practise skills, test equipment and run courses
in winter without cold, current or poor visibility.
