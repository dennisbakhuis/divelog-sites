A **30-hectare sand-extraction lake** between Delden and Bornerbroek in Overijssel, on the grounds of
camping 't Grasbroek. Maximum depth about **12 m**.

After the sand was taken out, local divers simply used it. A campsite and recreation lake grew up
around them, arrangements were made, and diving carried on — which is how most Dutch inland sites
came to exist. On any weekend it is full of clubs and courses.

Various things have been put on the bottom: an underwater obstacle course, a wreck that pike-perch
sit in, and the **Twents Onderwaterhuis** — the Arcadis underwater house — whose entrance is at
**11 m** and whose roof at **5 m** doubles as a training platform.
