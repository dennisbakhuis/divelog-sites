**Hin Muang** — *purple rock* — is 500 m from Hin Daeng. It never reaches the surface: the top of
the ridge is about 8 m down, which is why it stayed unknown until the 1980s despite being next to a
site people had been diving for years.

The ridge runs about 200 m and its walls drop past **70 m**, the deepest in Thailand. The name comes
from the dense cover of **purple soft coral** on the rock. Same cast as Hin Daeng — mantas, whale
sharks, grey reefs, big schooling fish — and the same hard current.
