The north-west coast here is **ironshore**: sharp, pitted, fossil limestone that is nearly
impossible to walk over in fins. At Nukove a **narrow cut** has been made through it, just wide
enough for one diver at a time, and that channel is the only reason the site is accessible at all.

Beyond it the reef has stands of **elkhorn coral** — a species that has been lost from most of the
Caribbean — and a drop-off close inshore.
