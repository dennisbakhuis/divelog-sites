One of the southern Dahab sites, reached by jeep down the coast road. The entry is a wide corridor
cut into a steeply sloping reef; the dive drops through it and goes around and under a large coral
tongue that hangs out over the slope.

Below that the reef gives way to sand, and the sand holds a colony of **garden eels** — a field of
them, retracting as divers approach and re-emerging behind them. Following the slope down past
**30 m** leads to large **gorgonian** fans, which mark the practical depth limit of the site.
