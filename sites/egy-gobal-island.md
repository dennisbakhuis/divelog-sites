Gubal Kebir (Big Gubal) and Gubal Seghir (Small Gubal) sit in the middle of the strait that takes
their name. They are low, bare, sandy islands with a lighthouse, and their value to divers is
mostly that they give shelter: the anchorages behind them are where the northern wreck boats spend
the night between the *Thistlegorm* and Abu Nuhas.

This site is **Bluff Point** on **Gubal Seghir** (Small Gubal), not Gubal Kebir. The reef in the
bay there is a shallow hard-coral garden on sand — undemanding, well lit and full of small life,
which suits it to the later, shallower dives of a day that starts on the wrecks.
