La Gabinière is a small rocky islet off the south coast of **Port-Cros**, inside the **Parc national
de Port-Cros** — created in 1963 and among the first marine national parks in Europe.

Decades of protection have produced what the site is known for: **dusky groupers**, *Épinephelus
marginatus*, in numbers and at size, sitting in the rocks and entirely indifferent to divers. The
species was fished nearly out of the western Mediterranean; Port-Cros is one of the places it came
back. Barracuda hang out in the blue off the wall.

The site is dived from **10 to 30 m**.
