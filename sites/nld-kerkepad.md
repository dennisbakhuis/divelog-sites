**Den Osse Kerkweg** is one of the three main dive sites on the Grevelingen, along with Scharendijke
and Dreischor Gemaal. The name *Kerkepad* (church path) is a variant of *kerkweg* (church road); no
dive site called Kerkepad exists in Zeeland, and the identification with Den Osse Kerkweg is
inferred rather than confirmed.

The site is on the **Grevelingen** near Brouwershaven: reef edges, old concrete slabs, reefballs and
the remains of a drowned dyke, in water with no tide and visibility that gets to twelve metres. It
is known for its biodiversity — dead man's fingers and the sea dahlia anemone among them — and it
works for beginners and for divers who want to go down the edge. Depths at the site reach **26 m**.
