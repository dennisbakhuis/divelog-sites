Anita's Reef runs from the south-eastern tip of Similan Island 6 around the east side of Island 5 —
the sheltered side, which makes it the opposite of the western boulder sites. A gently shelving
coral slope, white sand, granite boulders to the south and in the deeper water, little current and
excellent visibility.

The centrepiece is a single very large coral **bommie** standing on the sand, covered in soft coral
and full of fish. It is a standard last-dive-of-the-day site and also a standard
first-dive-of-the-trip site, for the same reason: nothing about it is difficult.
