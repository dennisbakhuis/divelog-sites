A **sand and gravel pit** in the floodplain of the Waal at Slijk-Ewijk in Gelderland, between
Nijmegen and Tiel. River-valley gravel extraction goes deep, and this one is one of the deepest
diveable waters in the country.

It is not a beginner's lake: the depth makes it a site for advanced and technical diving.
