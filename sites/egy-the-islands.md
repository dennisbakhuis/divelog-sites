South of the town at Laguna, the reef opens out into a complex of large **coral towers** standing
in white sand. The dive navigates between them along sand-floored corridors that open into small
amphitheatres and close again, most of it between **5 and 15 m**. The layout makes it one of the
few Red Sea sites with a maze-like, architectural character rather than a simple slope.

Yellowtail barracuda and twinspot snapper school in the gaps between the towers. The site is
shallow and sheltered, which allows long, unhurried dives.
