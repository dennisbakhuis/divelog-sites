Tajma Ha is a system in its own right, lying immediately north-west of **Sistema Minotauro** and
draining into it. Water sampling shows the two share the same water, but a collapse between them has
defeated every attempt to find a way through: sixty metres apart, hydrologically one cave,
cartographically two.

The cenote has several skylights that send strong beams of light through the fresh water in the
morning, a well-decorated cavern zone, and a halocline in the cave beyond.
