Ras Disha is the headland that shelters Makadi Bay from the north — 90 minutes south of Hurghada by
road, 25 minutes by boat from Makadi itself.

The dive has three parts. A fringing reef wraps the cape. Just south of it, three **ergs** rise
from sand at 10–12 m. And between the ergs and the reef there is a coral ledge at 12–15 m before
the drop-off. The northernmost erg has a small tunnel through it at about 5 m that is packed solid
with glassfish.
