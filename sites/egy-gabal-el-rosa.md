A bay on the coast south of Marsa Alam, of the standard southern type: a *marsa*, a drowned wadi
mouth, with a sandy seagrass floor inside and a reef across the entrance.

Sites of this kind in the deep south are
dived for what is on the seagrass (turtles, and with luck a dugong) rather than for the coral.
