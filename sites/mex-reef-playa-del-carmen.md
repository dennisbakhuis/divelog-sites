Offshore of Playa del Carmen the **Mesoamerican Barrier Reef** — the second largest reef system in
the world — runs north, and the **Yucatán Current** runs north over it strongly enough that every
dive on it is a drift dive. Divers enter, travel wherever the water takes them along the coral, and
the boat follows and picks them up wherever the drift ends.
