The Trou Madame rises at **Cénevières**, on the Lot. Dye tracing established that it is fed by the
**Charlan** sink at Vidaillac, up on the Causse de Limogne — the **Système du Charlan**.

The position was previously recorded at Saint-Sozy; the resurgence is at Cénevières,
50 km upstream on the Lot.
