The Giftun islands lie a few miles off Hurghada and are the reason the town could become a diving
resort at all: they break the prevailing northerly, and the strip of water behind them is diveable
almost every day of the year.

**Small Giftun** (Giftun Seghir) has a fringing reef with drop-offs on its exposed side and easy
coral gardens in its lee. By Red Sea standards it is a dependable site rather than a spectacular
one.

The Giftun islands are inside the Giftun–Ras Muhammad protected area and carry a per-person park
fee.
