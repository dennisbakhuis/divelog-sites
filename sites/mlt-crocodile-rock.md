Crocodile Rock lies between **Dwejra Point** and **Fungus Rock**, on the west coast of Gozo. The
name is what the rock looks like from the land — a crocodile's head lying in the water.

The dive works down a boulder slope and along a wall, so the depth can be matched to the plan; there
is a shallow reef on top, and the wall runs well past 40 m. The site is part of the same Dwejra
complex as the Blue Hole and the Inland Sea and is often the second dive of the day there.

**Fungus Rock** next door has its own history: the Knights of St John believed a plant growing on
its summit had medicinal value, declared the rock a reserve in the seventeenth century, cut the
sides smooth so nobody could climb it, and posted a guard.
