## The system

Mayan Blue, also called Escondido, opens into what was for years mapped as **Sistema Naranjal**.
On **28 March 2011** Steve Bogaerts, supported by Bil Phillips, made the connection that joined
Naranjal to **Sistema Ox Bel Ha** — *three paths of water* in Maya — and Naranjal stopped being a
system in its own right.

Ox Bel Ha is the largest of them all: **496.8 km** surveyed as of January 2023, more than 160
cenote entrances, over 56 000 survey stations, 2 500 jumps and 1 500 T-intersections. It was first
entered in 1996.

## The cave

The water above the halocline is stained dark by tannin from the surrounding jungle, and the walls
carry the stain in a band that marks where the fresh water sits. The halocline itself is at about
18 m. Below it the water is clear salt.

The good tunnels are B, E and F, all mostly in salt water. The **Battleship Room** is the set piece.
Three jumps from the main line get you across to **Cenote Naharón**; downstream connects to Cenote
Sun.
