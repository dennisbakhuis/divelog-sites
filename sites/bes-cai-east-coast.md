**Cai** is at the mouth of **Lac Bay**, on Bonaire's **east coast**. That side of the island faces
the trade winds and the open Atlantic swell, and it is not diveable most of the year. On the few
flat days it is, operators run trips over. The reef is unlike the leeward coast: heavier coral
structure built to take the surf, more surge, and few divers.

Lac Bay itself is a shallow lagoon behind a barrier reef, ringed with mangroves — a Ramsar wetland
and the nursery for a large fraction of the fish on the rest of the island.
