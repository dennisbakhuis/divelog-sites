A gravel pit at **Thorn** in Limburg, ten minutes from the Boschmolenplas and part of the same
Maasplassen chain.

Thorn itself is the *witte stadje*, a village of whitewashed brick that was for eight centuries a
sovereign imperial abbey ruled by women.
