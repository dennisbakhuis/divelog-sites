A group of very large granite boulders standing on a sand seabed directly **north of Ko Miang**,
Similan Island 4, in the centre of the archipelago. The rocks rise from **25 m and below** and the
site goes to 40 m, with soft corals and gorgonian sea fans lining the valleys between them. White
tip, black tip and leopard sharks are all regular.

Current can be strong, and it is a site for experienced divers.
