**Sistema Dos Pisos** is **14 176 m** long and **24.4 m** deep at its deepest, with five cenote
entrances: Balam, Dos Pisos, Dos Pisos Norte, **Monkey Dust** and Zooz.

The name — *two floors* — describes the cave: over much of its length the passage runs at two
distinct levels, with a decorated upper gallery and a lower one that runs through the halocline.

The cenote lies near Tulum, Quintana Roo, about 1.0 km along an unpaved track from MEX 307.
