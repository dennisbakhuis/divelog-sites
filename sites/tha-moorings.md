*Moorings* is where liveaboards tie up, not a named feature: the mooring field at **Ko Bangu**,
Similan Island 9.

Dives like this are the connective tissue of a liveaboard week — the check dive on arrival, the
dusk dive, the night dive, all done off the back of the boat on whatever reef happens to be under
it. The reef under this one is the sheltered eastern shelf of Island 9, which is the same water as
Breakfast Bend.

National park moorings exist so that boats do not anchor: dropping an anchor on Similan coral has
been prohibited since long before the 2018 restrictions.
