On the **north-west of Ko Similan**, Island 8 — the largest island in the group and the one with
Sail Rock on it. The site is a mix: a rocky side carrying coral communities, patches of reef to the
west, and a sand bottom at about 25 m, with the rocky area averaging 15–20 m.
