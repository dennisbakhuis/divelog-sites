Scuba-Academie runs a boat from the Baambrugse Zuwe at Vinkeveen out to any of the **Zandeilanden**,
the sand islands in the lake. This entry is that boat dive, named *Zandeiland 8 / 9* because that is
where it usually goes. Zandeiland 4 is the exception among the islands, being the one that can be
reached by car.

The position is the water between islands 8 and 9, rather than the departure point on the Baambrugse Zuwe.
