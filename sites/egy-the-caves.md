Down the southern coast road from Dahab. The reef wall here is undercut and pierced by a series of
small chambers and short swim-throughs at 10–20 m, none of them long enough to lose the light and
none of them requiring a line — caverns in the strict sense rather than caves.

The site is known for the light: the openings face west into the afternoon sun and the interiors
are full of glassfish, so the standard photograph is a silhouette against a blue hole in the rock.
