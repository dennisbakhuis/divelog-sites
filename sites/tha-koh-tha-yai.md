**Koh Haa Yai**, also recorded as *Koh Tha Yai*, is the largest of the five islets of **Koh Haa**
(*five islands*), between Phi Phi and Koh Lanta.

The main feature is **the Cathedral caves** on the south shore — chambers with an air space above
the water and **limestone stalactites** hanging from the roof, which is what happens when a karst
cave gets flooded by rising sea level and keeps its ceiling. **The Chimney**, the vertical
swim-through from about 4 m down to an exit at 17–18 m that often gets credited to Haa Yai, is at
**Koh Haa No. 1**, not here.

The lagoon in the middle of the five islets is sheltered enough to moor in and is the group's
other attraction.
