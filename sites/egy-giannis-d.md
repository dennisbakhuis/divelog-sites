## The ship

Built in 1969 by Kuryshima Dock Company at Imabari, Japan, as the **Shoyo Maru**: 99.5 m, 2 932
gross tons, a general cargo vessel with her engine and accommodation right aft. Sold in 1975 and
renamed **Markos**; sold again to Greek owners in 1980 and renamed **Giannis D**.

## The wreck

She sailed from Rijeka with sawn softwood for Jeddah and Hodeidah. In the early hours of
**19 April 1983**, running south through the Strait of Gubal, she went straight onto Abu Nuhas at
speed. Accounts put it down to a moment's inattention on the bridge. At 04:00 the master signalled
for the crew to be taken off; the hull had opened a twelve-metre tear. The Egyptian vessel *Santa
Fe* picked everyone up. Over the following days she worked herself off the reef and broke up.

## The dive

She lies in 10–28 m in three parts. The bow section sits more or less intact; the midships is a
flattened field of plating; and the **stern**, which is the reason people come, lies canted hard
over to port at around 45 degrees. Inside it the engine room, the workshop and the accommodation
are all penetrable and all tilted, so the stairs go sideways, the doorways are diamonds and every
horizontal surface lies. It is genuinely disorienting and it is the best-preserved machinery space
on Abu Nuhas. The mast still leans out over the sand.
