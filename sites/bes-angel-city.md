Angel City lies between **Alice in Wonderland** and the **Hilma Hooker** on the southern coast, and
it is often cited as the clearest example of the island's **double reef**: an inner reef from
about 5 m, a sand channel, and a second reef from about 18 m.

Because both reefs are shallow here, divers can cross the valley and come back without going deep,
which is why the site is recommended to newer divers and suits long, shallow dives.
