The **Gouffre de Padirac** is one of the great show caves of France: a 75 m open shaft into a
river cave, with boats running along an underground stream since 1899. The river could be seen
going in, but nobody could prove where it came out.

In **1947**, **75 kg of fluorescein** was poured into the Lac des Grands Gours, deep inside
Padirac. The dye appeared at the Fontaine Saint-Georges, on the Dordogne at Montvalent, on
**14 November** — **114 days** later. That single measurement established the **Système de
Padirac** and gave the region its first proof of how far and how slowly water moves under a causse.

The fountain itself is the diving entrance to the downstream end of that river.

plongeesout.ffspeleo.fr currently reports diving at Saint-Georges **prohibited pending works**.
