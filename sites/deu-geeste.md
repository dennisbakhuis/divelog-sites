The **Speicherbecken Geeste** is an artificial reservoir near Lingen in the Emsland, built as the
cooling-water store for the **Emsland power stations** — the Lingen nuclear plant and the gas-fired
units beside it. Water is taken from and returned to it rather than to the Ems directly.

Diving is on the reservoir itself: a shallow, flat, fresh-water basin with platforms and objects
placed on the bottom for training, and no current.

The Emsland reactor was shut down on **15 April 2023**, as one of the last three nuclear power
stations in Germany.
