**Golden Blocks** is a shore site south of Dahab. The entry leads down a sandy slope with an
extensive seagrass bed on the right at **16–20 m**, and the route swims out until two hard-coral
towers appear out of it.

The towers are a distinct warm gold colour, which is where the name comes from. A big gorgonian at
about **18 m** serves as the marker for finding them.
