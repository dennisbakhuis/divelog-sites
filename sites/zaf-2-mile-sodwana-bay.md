**Two-Mile Reef** is the largest and most dived of the Sodwana reefs — named, like all of them, for
how far it is up the coast from the launch at Jesser Point. It runs roughly 2 km by 900 m at depths
from 9 m to 35 m, and it is the main dive site of the whole area.

These are the **southernmost coral reefs in Africa**, kept alive at a latitude where they should not
survive by the warm **Agulhas Current** running down the coast. The species list is Indo-Pacific —
this is the western edge of the same reef fauna that runs all the way to the Pacific — but thinned
out and pushed to its limit.

There is no harbour. The boat is a rigid inflatable, it is pushed off the sand, and it goes out
through the surf line under full power with everyone holding the ropes. That is how every dive at
Sodwana starts.
