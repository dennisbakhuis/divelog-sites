A worked-out **limestone quarry** at Esneux, south of Liège in the valley of the Ourthe, flooded
when the face went below the water table. The rock is Carboniferous limestone — *petit granit*, the
blue-grey stone that faces half the buildings in Wallonia.

With almost no sea diving within reach, flooded pits like this are the nearest place for Dutch and
Flemish divers to do deep work in fresh water without a long drive, and La Gombe is one of the
standard deep-training quarries of the Low Countries.
