White Hole is on the **east coast**, near Cai, and like everything on that side it is only diveable
when the trade winds drop. It is a circular **bowl of white sand** in the reef, and its resident
population is a large school of **tarpon** — two-metre, silver-plated, prehistoric-looking fish
that hang in the bowl in formation and let divers come close.
