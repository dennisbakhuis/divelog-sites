**Koh Bida Nok** — *outer Bida* — is the larger and further out of two limestone pinnacle islands
south of Phi Phi Leh. The rock goes straight down from the waterline, and the dive is a circuit of
the island along a wall that bottoms out on sand at about 20–25 m.

The sand is the point. **Leopard sharks** (*Stegostoma tigrinum*) rest on it during the day, quite
still, and Bida Nok is the most reliable place in Thailand to find them. Blacktip reef sharks work
the shallows on the eastern side.
