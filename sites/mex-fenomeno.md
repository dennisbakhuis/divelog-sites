Fenomeno is reached from the 54 km mark on the Cancún–Tulum federal road, then about 8 km of dirt
track into the jungle. The entrance is a half-collapsed sinkhole with a **dilapidated bridge**
across it, which is also the clearest landmark on the track.

The permanent line drops to 10 m and runs about **1 600 m**. Two features on it stand out: an
**ancient vase** on the left-hand passage, and the bones of a **megatherium** — a giant ground sloth
— lying in a 17 m pit off the right-hand passage. Both lie where they fell, in a cave that was dry
at the time.

The cave connects to **La Concha** on the left and reaches **White River** at about 1 200 m.
