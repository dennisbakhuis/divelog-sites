**North Point**, also called Rocky Point, is the northern end of **Ko Bangu**, Similan Island 9,
and so the northernmost dive in the Similan group proper.

The granite here is stacked into narrow vertical gaps rather than the wide caverns of Elephant
Head: there are several **chimneys** to go up or down one diver at a time, the site's main
feature. Depths from 5 m to about 30 m, with current round the point.
