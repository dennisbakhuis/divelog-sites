One of the double-reef sites on the southern coast. The name refers to the **sand valley** between
the inner and outer reefs — a wide, flat, pale basin at around 18–20 m that on a still day looks
like the bed of a lake, and behaves like one: no surge, no current, nothing but garden eels and
rays on the sand.
