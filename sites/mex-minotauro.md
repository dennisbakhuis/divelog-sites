**Sistema Minotauro** is small and shallow — 17.7 m at its deepest — with entrances at Minotauro,
Escalera, Estrella, Piedra, Winz and one unnamed cenote.

Its main interest is the relationship with **Tajma Ha**, whose cenote lies about 600 m to the
north-west and whose nearest passages come within sixty metres of Minotauro's. Tajma Ha drains into
Minotauro, and water sampling shows it is the same water, but a collapse between the two has blocked
every attempt to connect them: two systems with one hydrology, separated by a rockfall.
