Tortuga was joined to the **Kim Ha** line in the summer of 2004. The combined cave has never been
given a formal *sistema* name, which is unusual and is why the `system` field here reads
**Tortuga–Kim Ha** rather than something official.

**2 488 m** of surveyed passage run from this entrance, to 28 m, with the halocline at 19 m. The
cenote shares an access road with Vaca Ha and Kim Ha.
