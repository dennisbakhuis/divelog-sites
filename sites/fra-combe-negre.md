An independent resurgence into the Dordogne at **Pinsac**, near Souillac, with no named system:
narrow passages and short siphons rather than a big flooded trunk.

The name is easily confused. There is a **Grotte de Combe Nègre** at Montbrun, 40 km away; there is
a locality called **La Combe Nègre** at Sauliac-sur-Célé; and there is another near Payrignac,
21 km away. The dive site is the **Source de Combe Nègre de Lanzac** at Pinsac, confirmed
independently by published GPS coordinates and by the Spéléo Club de Souillac's own videos of the
site.
