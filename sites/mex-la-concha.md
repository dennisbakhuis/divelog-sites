La Concha is entered through a narrow gateway in the rock that drops **straight to 10 m** — there is
no open basin at the surface, and the dive starts at depth. Maximum depth is only 10–12 m, and there
is roughly **2 500 m of line**.

The cave is decorated throughout with columns, stalagmites, stalactites and helictites, and unlike
many of the jungle cenotes the water is dependably clear rather than seasonally tannic. It connects
to **Fenomeno**.

Access is by the same track as Fenomeno, Nariz and White River: the 54 km mark on the Cancún–Tulum
federal road, then about 8 km of dirt track.
