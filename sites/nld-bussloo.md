**Bussloo** is a 150-hectare recreation lake at Voorst, between Apeldoorn and Zutphen in
Gelderland. It was created in the 1970s when sand was taken out for the **A1 motorway**, and the
area was then landscaped into a park with beaches, a naturist area and a dive site.

The water is fresh and shallow. There are platforms, sunken objects and a marked training area.
