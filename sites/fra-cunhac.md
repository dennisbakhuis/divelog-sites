Also called **Ressel 2**, but it is an independent siphon in the left bank of the Lot just below the **Saut de la Mounine**, with no named system
behind it.

The name is a person. **M. Cunhac** showed the source to the cave divers who explored it in
**1994**, and it has carried his name since.

The position was previously recorded as **Impasse de Cunhac** — a driveway 64 km east, matched on
the name alone.
