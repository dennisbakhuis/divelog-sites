The **Ouysse** is a river that spends most of its life underground. It sinks on the Causse de Gramat
east of Rocamadour and comes back up in two places at the foot of the plateau: here at **Cabouy**,
and 1.2 km west at the **Gouffre Saint-Sauveur**. **Font del Truffe**, further north-east, belongs
to the same basin.

Cabouy is a deep pool at the edge of a field, and it is the main diving entrance to the Ouysse
system. The passage runs back under the causse with substantial flow — this is a resurgence, so
divers swim in against the current and are carried out by it on the way back.
