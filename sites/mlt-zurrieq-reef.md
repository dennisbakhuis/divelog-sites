Żurrieq Reef is on **Malta** itself rather than Gozo. **Wied iż-Żurrieq** is the inlet on the south
coast that boats leave from to visit the **Blue Grotto**, the sea cave system in the cliffs where
the water lights up electric blue in the morning sun.

The reef off the inlet runs out from the shore with caves and an arch cut into the cliff base, in
10–25 m, and the water here is among the clearest around the islands.
