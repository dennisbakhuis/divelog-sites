**Karpata** is Papiamentu for the castor-oil plant, *Ricinus communis*, which grows thickly along
this stretch of the north-west coast. The dive site takes its name from the **Karpata plantation**
above it, whose landhuis still stands in ruins by the road — the estate grew aloe, and the stone
vats it was boiled in are still there.

The reef has a steep drop-off close in and dense hard coral. **Old anchors** lie on the slope,
left from the days when ships worked this coast.
