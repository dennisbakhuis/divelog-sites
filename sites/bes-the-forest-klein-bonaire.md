**Klein Bonaire** is an uninhabited limestone islet three-quarters of a mile off the west coast,
protected as a nature reserve since **2001**. It is boat-only, which means it gets a fraction of the
traffic of the mainland shore sites and the coral shows it.

The Forest is on the **south-west** side — the far side, exposed enough that it needs a calm day —
and it is named for a thick stand of **black coral** on the drop-off. Black coral is slow-growing
and was stripped out of most of the Caribbean for jewellery; a dense stand of it marks a reef that
has not been harvested.
