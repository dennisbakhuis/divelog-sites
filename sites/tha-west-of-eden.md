On the **west side of Ko Payu**, Similan Island 7, and named as the counterpart to East of Eden on
the other side of the island. Large boulders carrying giant **sea fans**, anemones, feather stars
and soft coral, with a rubble and reef slope falling to 30–35 m.

Being west-facing it takes the open Andaman swell and the current, which is why the coral is as
good as it is.
