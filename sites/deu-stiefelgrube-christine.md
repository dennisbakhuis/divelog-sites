Properly **Schiefergrube Christine**, at Willingen in the Upland. Four separate slate fields were
combined under the name *Christine* in **1863**; mining continued until **1971**, and when it
stopped the levels flooded.

Divers know it for the **miners' boots** left behind underground, which is where the name
*Stiefelgrube* — the boot mine — comes from. The flooded workings are dived along about **1.2 km of
guide line**, in water of around 6 °C.

Like Nuttlar this is a mine, not a karst cave, so no cave system applies. The entrance is on
Schwalefelder Strasse.
