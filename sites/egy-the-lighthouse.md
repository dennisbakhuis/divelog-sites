The Lighthouse is in the town, at the north end of the Masbat waterfront, and it is where much of
Dahab's diving activity takes place: open-water courses, night dives, freediving, equipment checks.
There is a shallow sandy bay with some coral in it for training, and then, about **fifty metres**
out, the reef edge and a **wall**.

That combination — a proper wall reachable directly from the waterfront — is a large part of the
reason Dahab became a diving town. The wall carries the usual Gulf of Aqaba life: hard coral on the
shoulder, gorgonians deeper, glassfish in the overhangs, and a wide diversity of fish.
