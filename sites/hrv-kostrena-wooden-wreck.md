Kostrena is a shore-diving site on the Kvarner coast just east of Rijeka, between the industrial
port and shipyards on one side and the **INA refinery** on the other. Despite the setting, the
water quality is unaffected, and the house reef is a **drop-off from about 11 m to 40 m**.

Low on that slope lies a **wooden wreck**, undated and, as far as published sources go,
unidentified. Wooden hulls do not survive in shallow Mediterranean water — teredo worm takes them —
so what is left is usually the buried lower hull, the frames, and whatever iron fastenings held it
together.
