Off the northern tip of **Ko Payu**, Similan Island 7. A field of pinnacles and boulder clusters
running from 5 m down to 40 m, heavily dressed in soft coral and sea fans.

It is generally rated among the top two or three sites in the archipelago, and it is the one where
the Similan pattern is clearest — the deeper the west-facing granite, the bigger the rocks and the
stronger the current.
