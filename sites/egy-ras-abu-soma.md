Ras Abu Soma is the cape that closes Soma Bay to the north, between Hurghada and Safaga. The reef
runs out from the point as a plateau at about 15–20 m before dropping off on both flanks, and
because it projects into open water it catches current — which is what brings the fish.

The northern side is the sheltered one, with coral gardens and ergs; the southern and eastern
faces are walls. Trevally and barracuda work the tip.
