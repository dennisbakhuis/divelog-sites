Marche-Pied sits on the same bank of the Célé as the Ressel, **2.5 km downstream**, and drains the
same **Réseau Gramat-Sud**. It is the Ressel's small, awkward neighbour.

There are three siphons, the third dropping into the **47 m** zone. The defining feature is the
entrance: for the first **40 m** the passage is too narrow to turn round in, so it is dived
**side-mount** and has to be committed to from the surface.
