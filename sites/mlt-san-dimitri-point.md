**San Dimitri Point** at San Lawrenz is the western end of Gozo, named for the chapel above it. It
is exposed to all weather and can only be dived on a calm day.

The cliff below is cut with a series of cracks and caverns, some big and long — **Whale Cave** and
**Tal-Barba Cave** both go in about 120 m, which puts them firmly beyond cavern diving. The
open-water side is a wall into deep blue water with pelagic fish passing, which is the main draw of
the site.
