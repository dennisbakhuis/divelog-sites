Between **November and March**, pregnant female **bull sharks** gather on the sand flats at 25–30 m
off Playa del Carmen. Why they choose this stretch is not fully settled — warm water, the fresh
groundwater discharging along the coast from the cenote systems, and proximity to pupping grounds
are all part of it — but they return every year, and Playa del Carmen has built a winter dive
industry on them.

The dives are done kneeling on the sand, in current, with a guide managing the animals. Whether the
sharks are baited is disputed locally: some operators feed them and some do not, and those that do
not argue that feeding changes the sharks' behaviour for everybody.
