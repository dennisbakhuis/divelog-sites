Chan Hol was connected to **Sistema Toh Ha** in March 2007.

It is best known outside diving for the **Chan Hol skeletons**. Several sets of Late Pleistocene
human remains have come out of this cave, including Chan Hol 2 and Chan Hol 3, and they belong to
the same story as the Eve of Naharón: people living in and around dry caves in the Yucatán more
than ten thousand years ago, before rising sea level flooded the passages and sealed everything in.
The finds have been central to the argument about when and by what route the Americas were first
settled — and, less happily, Chan Hol 2 was largely looted from the cave before it could be
properly studied.
