Shag Rock, sometimes listed as Shag Island, sits about five miles south of Sha'ab Ali and six from
the *Thistlegorm*. It is a large reef with a sandy plateau at 8–10 m and a permanent mooring, and
on its northern side at about 15 m lies the **Kingston**, wrecked there in 1881.

This entry covers the reef; the wreck has its own file at
`egy-kingston-shag-rock.md`.
