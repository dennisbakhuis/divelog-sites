The Abu Dabab name covers a run of numbered sites along the coast north of Marsa Alam. **Abu Dabab
II** is one of the offshore coral reefs rather than the dugong bay: pinnacles and hard-coral
gardens on sand in 10–20 m, with an overhang system on the seaward side.
