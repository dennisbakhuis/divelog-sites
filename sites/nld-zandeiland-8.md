**Zandeiland 8** is one of the spoil islands thrown up when the **Vinkeveense Plassen** were dug
for motorway sand. Islands 8 and 9 sit in the northern lake and are reached by boat — usually the
Scuba-Academie boat out of the Baambrugse Zuwe.

The islands are numbered *eiland-1* to *eiland-12*, and the position here is *eiland-8*.
The *eiland-4* position agrees with the road-access Zandeiland 4 to within 80 m.
