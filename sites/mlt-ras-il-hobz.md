Ras il-Ħobż — *bread cape* — is at **Mġarr ix-Xini** on the south coast of Gozo: a shore dive onto a
single **pinnacle** that rises from more than 40 m of water to about 10 m below the surface. Divers
call it the **Middle Finger**, for reasons that are clear from the profile.

The narrow channel between the pinnacle and the shore bottoms out at **35 m**, so the standard dive
runs down the channel, round the base and up the tower in a spiral. The site is a single object,
visible as a whole, and the depth of the dive is entirely up to the diver.
