Landenouse rises into the Lot about 2 km from **Cajarc**, below the D662. It belongs to no named
system: it is a resurgence of the causse in its own right, and it has not yet been connected to
any other cave.

As of 2024 it has **2 134 m** of surveyed passage and reaches **122 m** deep. The shallow entrance
section lies at about **19–23 m**.

The stored position is the GPS from the 2021–2024 cave survey.
