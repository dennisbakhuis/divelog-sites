South-east of Hurghada, a shallow reef of coral pillars and table corals standing on brilliant
white sand, with a maximum depth of about **15 m** across the whole site. Everybody calls it **the
Aquarium**, and the nickname fits: the towers hold enormous standing shoals of glassfish, anthias
and goatfish, with bannerfish and Napoleon wrasse working through them.

Because it is shallow, sheltered and safe it is the classic second or third dive of the day, and
dives on it tend to run long.
