A limestone terrace on the north-west coast below the Rincon road, reached by a flight of concrete
steps cut into the cliff. Depending on who is counting there are **sixty-seven**, or seventy-two —
never a thousand. The name is a joke about the climb back out.

The reef below is a broad shallow terrace of hard coral, then a drop-off, with enough space to swim
a long way in either direction. Turtles are routine.
