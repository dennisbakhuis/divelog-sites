Not a named dive site but a position: a point on the wall about two kilometres south of
**Gabr el Bint**, reached by carrying on past that site on the same boat.

That works on this coast because the wall is continuous: the Gulf of Aqaba is a flooded transform
fault and the reef runs unbroken along the fault scarp for tens of kilometres, so any point on it
is a wall dive. What varies is how many divers have been there, and two kilometres past the last
named site the answer is close to none.
