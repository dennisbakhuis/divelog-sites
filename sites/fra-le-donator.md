## The ship

Built at Bergen in **1931** as the *Donator*. In 1933 the **Compagnie Algérienne de Navigation pour
l'Afrique du Nord** — the Schiaffino company — bought her and renamed her *Prosper Schiaffino*,
after a member of the family, as was their habit.

The company lost every one of its ships during the Second World War. The *Donator* was the only
survivor.

## The sinking

On **10 November 1945**, six months after the fighting in Europe stopped, she was on her way back
from Algeria with a cargo of **wine**, having taken out dried vegetables and potatoes. At 13:15, in
the channel between Port-Cros and Porquerolles, she hit a **drifting mine**. It nearly cut her in
two and she went down before the boats could be launched. **Five men died.**

## The dive

She sits upright on the sand between **35 and 52 m**, and she is one of the most highly regarded
wrecks in the Mediterranean — big, intact enough to be legible, and deep enough to be a proper
undertaking. Current here can be strong.
