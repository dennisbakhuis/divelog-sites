*Umm Gamar* means **mother of the moon**, for the crescent shape of the small island that breaks
the surface at the top of it. The dive is on the rest of the mountain: an isolated reef rising
from deep water, roughly T-shaped, about **90 minutes north of Hurghada** by boat.

The **southern plateau** at **14–24 m** is the usual dive — a coral garden with turtles, batfish,
Napoleon wrasse and morays, and enough small ergs to keep the navigation interesting. To the west
the plateau is shallower and broken into little sub-reefs. The northern end falls away into
current and depth.
