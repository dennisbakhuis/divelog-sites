## The exploration

Nohoch Nah Chich was found on **26 November 1987**. **Mike Madden** of CEDAM International led the
first survey through the summer of 1988, and from 1989 to 1996 ran expeditions of two weeks to a
month at a time, pushing and mapping it. Through most of the 1990s Nohoch was **the longest
surveyed underwater cave in the world**, and it was the site that made Quintana Roo the centre of
cave exploration.

It lost the title in the way these things always go — not to a longer cave, but to a connection.
Nohoch was linked to **Sistema Sac Actun**, and Sac Actun, having also swallowed Dos Ojos in
January 2018, is now the longest underwater cave system on Earth at over 370 km.

## The cave

Shallow and heavily decorated, with the white formations and clear fresh water that Nohoch is
photographed for. It is the classic Quintana Roo postcard cave.
