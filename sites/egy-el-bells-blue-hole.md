**El Bells** is a narrow vertical chimney cut through the reef about 500 m north of the Blue Hole.
Divers walk in off the shore, fin out a few metres, and descend into a shaft barely wider than a
diver with gear on. It runs from about 3 m down to **27 m**, where it opens abruptly out of the
side of the cliff into blue water with the wall dropping away beneath. The transition from a stone
tube to unlimited open water is the whole point of the site.

From there the route turns left and drifts south along the wall — big gorgonians, a shallow ledge
of coral on the way — until the wall breaks and the dive comes over the **saddle** into the Blue
Hole and exits on the beach.

The traverse is one dive. Unlike the Arch route at the Blue Hole, it spends its deepest part at
the very beginning and gets progressively shallower.
