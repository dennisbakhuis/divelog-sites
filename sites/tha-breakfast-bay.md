**Breakfast Bend**, also recorded as *Breakfast Bay*, is on the **east side of Ko Bangu**, Similan
Island 9. It is the sheltered side: a gentle slope of hard coral, soft coral and sea fans from about
5 m to 30 m, no current to speak of, and the reef running north along the coast for a long way.

The name is operational. Liveaboards moored at Island 9 overnight dive it as the first dive of the
day, before breakfast. Three Trees and North Point are on the same island.
