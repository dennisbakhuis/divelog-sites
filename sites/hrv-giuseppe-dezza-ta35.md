## The ship

Launched in **1916** as the Italian destroyer **Pilade Bronzetti**, of the Rosolino Pilo class. In
1929 she was renamed **Giuseppe Dezza**. After the Italian armistice of September 1943 the Germans
took her over, rebuilt her as a torpedo boat, and recommissioned her as **TA35** in the
Kriegsmarine.

## The sinking

On **17 August 1944**, in the Fažana channel between Pula and Rovinj, she hit a mine. She broke in
two. **Seventy-one men died.**

## The dive

The two halves lie about **70 m apart** in around 34 m of water, far enough that it takes two dives
to see both. The larger **stern section sits upright** on the flat bottom; the **bow lies on its
port side**.

The armament is still there and is the reason people dive her: two **10.2 cm L/45 guns**, and two
**20 mm quadruple mounts** — one gun platform has fallen over, the other is still in place with the
barrels sticking out. She is shallow enough and open enough to be a straightforward dive.
