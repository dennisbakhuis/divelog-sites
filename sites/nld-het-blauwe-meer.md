't Blauwe Meer at **Loon op Zand** is run by the Dive Company and is not open for walk-in diving:
diving is **by appointment and reservation only**.

Clear, pale-blue fresh water with visibility usually between 5 and 15 m, and a maximum depth of
about **24–28 m**. Big shoals of perch, large eels, pike and carp. On the bottom there is a
**Cessna** aircraft wreck, a flexible underwater training rig, and **Wreck Alley** — a line
connecting six wrecks. There is a beach entry and a *duiktaxi* to run divers out to different parts
of the lake.

Not to be confused with **Het Blauwe Meer in Dinxperlo**, an open-air swimming pool 0.80 to 1.40 m
deep.
