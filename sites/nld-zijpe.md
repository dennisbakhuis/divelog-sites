The **Zijpe** is the tidal channel between Schouwen-Duiveland and Sint Philipsland, at Bruinisse.
It connects the Oosterschelde to the Krammer-Volkerak and it runs fast — this is a slack-water dive
with a tight window, and the reward is the life that current-swept hard substrate supports.
