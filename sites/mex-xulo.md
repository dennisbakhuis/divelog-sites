Xulo lies about 200 m off the highway south-west of Tulum, near Muyil. It is one of four cenotes
belonging to **Sistema Caterpillar**, which has **13 452 m** of surveyed passage; **1 372 m** of it
is reached from this entrance, and it connects to Cenote Caterpillar.

Tunnel A runs through a pronounced **halocline**: hazy and brown-tinted in the fresh water above,
clear below. Passing through it is like entering a different cave.

The lines include a main line with three short circuits near the entrance, a loop and an east–west
line at the far end, a jump to the Caterpillar traverse with the Pluto–Neptune line off it, and an
east loop that ends in a **no-mount restriction**.
