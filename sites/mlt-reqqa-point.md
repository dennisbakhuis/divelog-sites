**Reqqa Point** is the northernmost tip of Gozo, near Marsalforn, and offers the island's most
serious shore dive. The wall drops to 40 m and beyond; a rocky shelf at about 18 m holds jacks,
morays, damselfish and dense shoals of sardines.

On the western side is **Bottleneck Cave**, a vertical cylinder about 5 m across dropping through
the reef from **37 m to 58 m**, with cracks along its walls full of shrimp. That part of the site is
technical diving territory.
