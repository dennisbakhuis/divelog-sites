Off **Xwejni Bay** on the north coast. The reef here carries a formation of **two arches, one above
the other**, cut into a rock mass standing off the main wall — the upper arch at around 18 m and the
lower opening at about 35–40 m.

It is a long surface swim out from the bay, or a boat drop, and the depth means the bottom arch is
a short visit.
