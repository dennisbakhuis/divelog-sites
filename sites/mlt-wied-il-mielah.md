**Wied il-Mielaħ** is a valley on the north coast at Għasri that ends in a **natural limestone
arch** over the sea, reached down a long flight of steps and a concrete platform.

Since the Azure Window collapsed in March 2017, this is the surviving natural arch on Gozo, and it
has taken over much of the dive traffic. The dive goes out under the arch and along the wall, which
is steep, cut with caves and crevices, and deeper than most of the shore sites.
