Abu Dabab is a wide sandy bay about 30 km north of Marsa Alam whose floor is a continuous meadow of
**seagrass** in 5–12 m of water. That meadow supports large green turtles in numbers, and the
animal people actually come for: **dugongs**. This is the most reliable place in the Red Sea to see
one, and it is still not reliable.

The bay is inside a nationally protected area and the protection has real teeth. Boats are no longer
allowed into the bay; the moorings that let safari boats stay overnight were removed; parts of the
seagrass are closed to divers and snorkellers entirely; and rangers patrol it. The rules exist
because the animals were being crowded off their own feeding ground.
