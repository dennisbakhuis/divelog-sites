A small flooded pit at **Neuenkirchen**, just over the border from Twente, and
properly called the **Offlumer See**.

It is one of several flooded pits on the German side of the border, alongside Dankern, Lathen and
Geeste, that are within easy reach of divers from Overijssel and Twente.
