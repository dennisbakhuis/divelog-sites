On the north-west coast, between **Karpata** and **Nukove**. It is not one of the 65 Bonaire dive
sites carried in OpenStreetMap and it is missing from several of the printed site lists, which
suggests it is either a local name for a stretch of reef between two marked sites, or a marker
that has come and gone.

The coast here is ironshore terrace with a steep drop-off close in, and it is among the least
visited diving on the island because it is a long drive from town.
