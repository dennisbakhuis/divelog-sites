One of the southern shore sites. A sandy slope runs down to about 10 m, then a shallow drop-off at
18 m, and between them the bottom is scattered with coral blocks of various sizes standing free on
the sand. The largest of them, at about 14 m, is the set piece. Average depth is around 15 m and
there is little reason to go past 25.

The name is accurate: the blocks are riddled with holes and the holes have **moray eels** in them,
often several to a block, and often out and moving in daylight.
