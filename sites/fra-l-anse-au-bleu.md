A rocky drop-off running from about **14 m to 26 m**, with a **Posidonia** seagrass flat on the
shoulder above it, dived by boat out of Giens.

The name is sometimes written *L'Anse au Bleu* — blue cove — but the name on the charts is
**L'Anse au Blé**, wheat cove, which is how it is recorded here.

The *Posidonia oceanica* meadow is significant. It is a flowering plant rather than a seaweed,
endemic to the Mediterranean, it grows a few centimetres a year, and the mats it builds can be
thousands of years old. It is also the nursery for most of what lives on the rock next to it.
