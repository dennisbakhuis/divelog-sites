**Anna Jacobapolder** is the old ferry harbour on the Sint Philipsland side of the **Krammer**. The
dive at this site is a boat dive on the **Veenplaten**, the peat banks off the harbour at 5–8 m, run from
the dive boat **Tertius**, which also runs boat dives at Bruinisse.

Despite the name of the entry, there is no wreck called *Tertius* here: Tertius is the name of the
dive boat.
