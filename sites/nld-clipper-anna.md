**Klipper Anna** is a 24-metre sailing ship out of **Zierikzee** that runs dive weeks on the
Zeeland waters, on the Grevelingen and the Oosterschelde.

This entry covers boat dives made from the ship rather than one fixed spot. Where she anchors is not
recorded, so the site is placed in the middle of the **Grevelingenmeer** and shown by region only.
