A *Freizeitsee* — literally a leisure lake — at **Lathen** in the Emsland, another of the flooded
extraction pits that the Ems valley is full of.

Lathen's other claim on the world is above ground: the **Transrapid** magnetic-levitation test
track ran through the village from 1984 until the 2006 accident that killed 23 people and
effectively ended the programme.
