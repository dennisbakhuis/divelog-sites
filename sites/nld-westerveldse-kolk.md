A *kolk* is not an extraction pit. It is the scour hole left behind when a dyke bursts: the water
coming through cuts a deep round pit on the inland side, and when the breach is repaired the hole
stays. The Dutch river landscape is dotted with them, each one the memorial of a flood.

The **Westerveldse Kolk** is on the north side of Zwolle.
