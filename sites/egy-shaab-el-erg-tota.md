*Shaab el Erg Tota* is a named spot within the Shaab el Erg reef system rather than a separate
reef. *Tota* is used locally for a small coral tower, and the lagoon inside the horseshoe is full
of them.

The regional context is in `egy-shaab-el-erg.md`.
