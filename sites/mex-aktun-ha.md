*Aktun Ha* is Maya for **water cave**. The other name is more prosaic and more used: the cenote sits
right beside the Tulum–Cobá road, and for years taxi drivers pulled in and washed their cars in it.

It is one of the standard **training cenotes**: an open pool with a shallow ledge, a resident
crocodile in some seasons, and a cavern zone lit well enough to teach in. The cave behind it is the
downstream end of **Sistema Aktun Ha**, whose upstream entrance is **Zacil Ha**, about 200 m along
the road and reachable along the main line.
