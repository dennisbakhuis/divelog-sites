**Brayka Bay** is a resort bay on the Red Sea coast north of Marsa Alam, also known as the Brayka
Bay House Reef. The bay encloses a sheltered sandy lagoon, with a fringing reef on each of its
arms.

The two sides differ. The **northern reef** drops off properly into deeper water. The **southern
side** is shallower and carries seagrass. As the house reef of the resort on the bay, it is dived
from the shore, which makes it a common choice for check dives and night dives.
