This entry is a placeholder and is flagged as such.

There is no dive site called *Wave* at Hurghada. The name is much more likely to be that of a boat
than of a location, as happens when a dive is logged by vessel rather than by site.

The position is set on the **El Mina** — an Egyptian minesweeper sunk by Israeli aircraft in 1970,
lying just outside Hurghada harbour at about **30 m**, which is the standard final dive for boats
coming back into Hurghada. The location should be treated as unconfirmed.
