**Hin Daeng** — *red rock* — is an isolated pinnacle in open water about 70 km south-west of Koh
Lanta, with nothing around it. A small piece of it breaks the surface; the rest drops away steeply
on all sides past 60 m.

The name comes from the **red and pink soft corals** that cover the rock from the surface down.
Because it stands alone in deep water it acts as a magnet: **manta rays**, and the best chance in
Thailand of a **whale shark**, along with grey reef sharks and large schools of trevally and
barracuda.

It is not a beginner's site. There is no shelter, the current runs hard, and the boat ride is three
hours each way.
