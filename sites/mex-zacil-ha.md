Zacil Ha is a small developed cenote on the Tulum–Cobá road (QR 109), with a swimming platform and a
zip line. Behind the pool it is the **upstream end of Sistema Aktun Ha**, and the main line runs
from here through to the **Car Wash** entrance about 200 m away. The traverse between the two
entrances is the main cave dive from this site.
