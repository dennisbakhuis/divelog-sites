White River is a cenote in the same cave as **Fenomeno**, about **700 m** (2 300 ft) along the line
from the Fenomeno entrance. It has its own opening to the surface in the jungle, about 450 m west
and 400 m north of the Fenomeno entrance, and the site is mapped at that opening.

Access is by the track shared with Fenomeno, La Concha and Nariz: leave MEX 307 at the 54 km mark
and follow about 8 km of dirt track.
