*Heart of paradise*, also known as **Chuup Ich**. The cenote was connected to **Sistema Ox Bel Ha**
by Bil Phillips and Steve Bogaerts between 2001 and 2003.

The cave's character comes from the marshland above it. Tannins leaching from the marsh stain the
fresh water dark brown-black, so the upper part of the cave is a tea-coloured gloom. The **halocline
sits at about 16 m**, and the salt water below it is brilliantly clear and reflects white off pale
limestone. Below the boundary the cave looks like a different one.
