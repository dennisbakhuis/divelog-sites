The south coast of Bonaire has something the rest of the island does not: a **double reef**. An
inner reef starts at about 5 m and slopes down to a **sand valley at 18–20 m**; out across the
sand, a **second reef** rises again and drops away into deep water.

Alice in Wonderland is the site named for it. The first reef is the one most divers visit;
reaching the second reef means crossing the sand valley and needs extra planning. The outer reef
is less visited, in better condition, and has the bigger animals.
